About Us

A favour that became a craft.

Shine O Shine began quietly — a Chennai homemaker asked, on behalf of interior design clients, whether the tarnished brass and silver gathering dust in their homes could be brought back to life. What started as a one-off favour turned into a full-time vocation, and then into a workshop that now restores over 100 kilograms of metalware every week.

Two decades on, families across Kerala and Tamil Nadu still send us the pieces that matter most — ancestral urulis, wedding silver, trophies, temple lamps — and we've earned the trust of collectors, hoteliers and antique dealers along the way, with our story covered in the national press.

We don't believe in stripping a piece back to factory-new. Patina is history — our job is to remove what's damaging the metal, and protect what makes it genuinely yours.

Esteemed Clientele

Collectors, film families and public figures across Chennai have trusted us with their finest pieces — as reported in the national press.

What We Restore

Three metals, three different disciplines.

Each metal tarnishes, corrodes and ages in its own way — so each gets its own treatment, matched to the piece in front of us rather than a one-size routine.

Silver Restoration

Gentle tarnish removal that's kind to plating, with a protective finish that keeps the shine honest for longer.

Typically:
Tea sets · Fine cutlery · Trophies · Religious icons · Heirloom jewellery

Brass & Bronze

Old lacquer and heavy tarnish lifted away, then finished to a high gloss or a soft hand-rubbed satin — your call.

Typically:
Traditional urulis · Lamps · Door hardware · Musical instruments · Statues

Copper Artifacts

Active corrosion treated at the source, then finished either to a mirror shine or a carefully preserved antique patina.

Typically:
Cookware · Architectural accents · Antique sinks · Decorative vessels

Good to Know

Questions we hear often.

Will polishing reduce the value of my antique?

+

Only if it's done carelessly. We work to remove active rust, verdigris and damaging tarnish while protecting the historical character of the piece — value comes from that balance, not from over-polishing.

Can you handle large or heavy items?

+

Yes — oversized urulis, temple lamps and even commercial-scale installations are part of our regular week's work.

How long does the shine actually last?

+

All metal re-tarnishes naturally over time, but our finish is built to slow that down considerably compared to an off-the-shelf polish.

Do I need to bring the item anywhere?

+

No — we collect from your home or office in Chennai, and deliver the finished piece back to you, all within two weeks.
