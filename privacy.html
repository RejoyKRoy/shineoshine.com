Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what information Shine O Shine collects when you use our website or contact us for a restoration quote, and how we use it. We're a small Chennai-based workshop, and we keep our data practices simple and limited to what's needed to serve you.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following, generally when you reach out to us directly:

  • Your name, phone number and WhatsApp number.
  • Your address, for item collection and delivery.
  • Photographs of items you send us for a quote or restoration.
  • Basic details about the item — metal type, weight, condition.
  • Any email address you use to contact us.

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect payment card details directly through this website.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide accurate quotes and estimates.
  • To arrange collection and delivery of your items.
  • To communicate with you about the status of your restoration.
  • To keep basic records for our own quality and accounting purposes.
  • With your permission, to feature before-and-after photos of restored items on our Instagram or website.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited details with:

  • Delivery or logistics partners, solely to arrange collection and drop-off.
  • Service providers who help us run the website (for example, hosting), where required for the site to function.

We do not share your information with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

4. WhatsApp & Communication

Most of our customer communication happens over WhatsApp and phone calls, which are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy practices in addition to ours. Messages and photos you send us are kept only as long as needed to complete your order and for basic record-keeping afterward.

5. Cookies & Website Analytics

This website may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand how visitors use the site (for example, which pages are viewed). We do not use this information to identify individual visitors personally.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices

You can ask us at any time to:

  • Not feature your item's photos publicly.
  • Delete personal information we hold about you, where we're not required to retain it for legal or accounting reasons.
  • Update incorrect contact details we have on file.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults arranging restoration on their own behalf, or on behalf of a business, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

10. Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or requests, reach us at hello@shineoshine.com or WhatsApp.
